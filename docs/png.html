Leadtrigger for Connect Group: instructions and walkthroughs

This page explains how Connect Group sales people use Leadtrigger. The first part is the rep sheet. The second part is a set of walkthroughs with screenshots. The labels match the app as of 25 September 2026. Some screenshots were taken earlier and can show older wording; when they disagree, the text is right.

Screenshots show the admin view, and some are older than the current app. Reps see only Connect Group (home), Share lists, Browse companies and Prepare one.

Part 1: Rep sheet

Quick start

  1. Log in. Use your email and your own password.
  2. Click "Browse companies". It's under Sales in the menu.
  3. Pick a country or sector, then click "Search". Nothing changes until you click Search. If the Search button blinks orange, click it. Tip: "High fit" shows the best matches first.
  4. Click "Open" on a company. This opens the company's page. "Not ready" means there's no page yet.
  5. Answer 3 quick questions, then get the PDF. Answer "Agree" or "Don't agree" for the fit, the urgency and the coaching notes. Click "Save my answers", then "Download PDF". The button stays grey until your answers are saved. You send the PDF yourself, because nothing gets emailed for you.

No page yet? - Tick the company and click "Create pages". New pages are drafts, so switch the Article menu to "Show not ready too" and click Search to see them. - Can't find the company? Click "Prepare one", fill in "Company name" and "Domain" (for example embat.io), and click "Prepare outreach". The page takes a few minutes to build, so wait a bit before you click "Open article".

Stuck? Ask the "Sales help" chat on the Browse page, for example "What should I click?"

Every box explained

The menu on the left

  • Connect Group: your home page. It describes what you sell. You can ignore the "Quick links" box there, because those are admin tools.
  • Share lists: bundles of company pages you can send as one link.
  • Browse companies: find companies and open their pages. This is where you'll spend most of your time.
  • Prepare one: build a page for one company that isn't in the list yet.

Browse companies

Left side, "Start from companies" (each button searches straight away): - Companies with articles: companies that already have a page, finished or draft. - Full company universe: every company, with or without a page. - Companies without articles: companies that don't have a page yet. - Prepare one company: goes to Prepare one.

Left side, "Action boxes": - Look for a company: puts your cursor in the Search box, so you can type a name. - Look for companies in a region: opens the Region picker. - High fit: shows companies with a fit of 70 or more, best first. - High urgency: shows companies with an urgency of 70 or more, most urgent first. - What does Jev say?: asks for a grade on the companies you ticked. Tick them first, and tick 100 at most. It starts right away.

The Search button. Your filters only apply after you click it. It blinks orange when you've changed something and haven't searched yet.

The filter boxes: - Search: type a company name or website. - Region / Country: pick a region, or one country. Country is greyed out while a region is picked. - Sector: the type of business. You can also click a sector in the table to set it. - Fit min / Urgency min: only keep companies at or above this number (0 to 100). Companies without a score drop out. - Jev min: this filters on how sure Jev is (0 to 100), not on the letter. For the letter, use "Jev grade". - Jev grade: Any, "A — best", B, or "A or B". - Article: "Ready only" (the default) shows only finished pages. "Show not ready too" shows everything. "With article" shows any page, including drafts. "No article" shows companies without a page. - Watchlist: only companies on one of your saved lists. - CSV upload: only companies from one uploaded file. It says "No uploads yet" when there aren't any. - CRM: "In CRM" or "Not in CRM", meaning whether the company is already in Connect Group's customer list. - More filters: extra filters you can ignore (Similarity, Embeddable only, Site visitor, CG BU, CG class, CG origin). The one that's useful is Site visitor, which shows companies that visited your website. - Clear filters: resets everything.

The table: - Page: "Open" opens the company page in a new tab. "Not ready" means there's no page yet. - Fit: how well the company matches what Connect Group sells (0 to 100). It's green at 70 or more and yellow at 50 or more. - Urgency: how good the timing is to reach out now (0 to 100). It's red at 70 or more and orange at 40 or more. On this page, red is good. - Jev: a letter grade from A (best) to E. A green A or B is a strong pick. "A review" or "B review" in yellow means Jev wasn't sure, so check the company yourself. "unclear" means the grade didn't work. "…" means it's still running. - Sector, Country, CRM: plain facts about the company. - Visitor, Lead, Noul, SIM: extra columns you can ignore. Click "Hide score details" to hide them. - Row colour: green means high fit, pink means high urgency, and amber means both.

When you tick companies, a bar appears with these buttons: - Create pages: makes draft pages for the ticked companies that don't have one yet. It only works when at least one ticked company says "Not ready". Remember that "Ready only" hides drafts, so switch to "Show not ready too". - Save as watchlist: saves the ticked companies as a named list. - Clear: unticks everything.

Save filtered (up to 1500) appears once you've set a filter. It saves everything your filters found (up to 1,500 companies) as a new watchlist plus a share list, and opens the watchlist. The name is filled in for you.

The company page (after you click "Open")

  • The status at the top: "Ready" means the page is finished. "Fit done", "Urgency done" and "Outreach done" mean it's partly built. "Needs research" means it isn't built yet, is out of date, or failed.
  • The three step buttons: "1 Research Fit", "2 Research Urgency" and "3 Create Outreach" fill the page step by step. "Find decision-makers" looks for contacts. "Research Urgency" stays greyed out until the company has a website.
  • The tabs (they jump to parts of the page):
  • 01 Why now: the reason to contact them now, with sources.
  • 02 Your Brief: one question, "Is this urgency right?", which you answer with Too low, About right or Too high. This is not the PDF check.
  • 03 What to say: a ready opener, plus an email, a LinkedIn note and a call script once outreach is made.
  • 04 Contacts: people at the company, which you can filter by level and department.
  • 05 Fit: why the company fits, scored piece by piece.
  • 06 Evidence trail: every claim with its source.
  • 07 Coaching: what to do next.
  • A tab marked "Empty" isn't filled yet.
  • Score tiles: Fit, Timing, Priority (fit and timing combined) and Employees. Here they're green at 80 or more, amber at 60 or more, and blue below that.
  • Check this brief: the three Agree / Don't agree questions, then "Save my answers" and "Download PDF".
  • Do you agree with this brief? (the box labelled "Your take", further down the shared page): three quick checks on the company. Is the fit analysis right, is the urgency right, and are the coaching notes right? Each gets Agree, Unsure or Disagree. You can add a comment, your name and your email, which are all optional. Then click "Send answers". After that the button reads "Update answers", so you can change them later. This box is separate from "Check this brief": it doesn't unlock the PDF, and it has an extra "Unsure" choice. Use "Check this brief" to get your PDF, and use this box to tell us what's wrong with a page.
  • Share link (a small box next to Coaching): the page's web address. Click it to copy it, then paste it into your own email or chat.

Share lists

  • Open: opens the list's public page in a new tab.
  • Link: copies the link, so you can paste it into your own email.
  • Delete: removes the list for good. Only use it on lists you made. The app doesn't stop you from deleting someone else's list, so be careful.
  • You can ignore "RSS" and "JSON", which are technical exports.
  • On the public list page, the Fit and Why-now colours are different from Browse. There, green is 80 or more, amber is 60 or more, and red means low.

Prepare one

  • Company name and Domain (the website, for example embat.io).
  • Prepare outreach: finds or adds the company and starts building its page. It takes about 2 to 5 minutes.
  • Open article: opens the page. Give it a few minutes first.

Words you'll see

  • Fit: how well a company matches what Connect Group sells (0 to 100).
  • Urgency: how good the timing is to reach out now (0 to 100). The same score is called Why-now on share lists and Timing on the company page.
  • Jev: an automatic grade from A (best) to E for how well a company fits Connect Group. The Jev number is how sure it is, not the grade.
  • Ready / Draft / Not ready: Ready means the page is finished. Draft means it has started but isn't finished, and "Ready only" hides drafts. Not ready means there's no page yet.
  • Page, brief, article: these all mean the same thing, the one-company page you open and turn into a PDF.
  • Watchlist vs share list: a watchlist is a saved group of companies you work on. A share list is a link to a bundle of company pages that you can send to someone.

Three things that trip people up

  1. Colours change between pages. On Browse, red urgency means high. On the share list page, red means low.
  2. "Create pages" makes drafts, and the default "Ready only" hides them. Switch to "Show not ready too".
  3. "Jev min" filters on how sure Jev is, not on the letter. To filter on the letter, use "Jev grade".

Part 2: Walkthroughs

Open the Offering Company Finder and pick "Companies with articles"

The Offering Company Finder is at the web address ending in /companies/start. It has no link in the menu, so you open it by typing that address. Its heading reads "Connect Group · Offering Company Finder". The first card, "Companies with articles", opens Browse companies showing only companies that already have a page.

Browse the full company universe

The second card on the Offering Company Finder, "Full company universe", opens Browse companies with every company, with or without a page. Some rows will say "Not ready", and that is expected.

The action boxes on Browse companies

When you click an action box, the filter it controls gets a green ring so you can see what changed. When you click one of the "Start from companies" buttons, the filter it sets gets an amber ring and the search runs straight away.

The Manual settings box

The filter boxes (Search, Region, Country, Sector, Fit min, Urgency min, Jev min, Jev grade, Article, Watchlist, CSV upload and CRM) sit together in one box on Browse companies. Change what you need, then click "Search".

The list stays visible while it updates

When you search again, the old rows stay on screen and the page shows "Updating…" until the new results arrive. The list doesn't go blank.

Open a company page in a new tab

In the Page column, "Open" opens the company page in a new browser tab. You stay on Browse companies in the first tab.

The same Fit score on the list and on the page

A company's Fit is the same number on Browse companies and on its page. In this example Soltec has a Fit of 84 in both places.

The Employees tile

The Employees tile at the top of the company page shows how many people work there. When the number isn't known, the tile says so. The tile also fits on a phone screen.

Jump to a part of the page with the tabs

The numbered tabs stay at the top while you scroll. Clicking one, for example "05 Fit", "01 Why now" or "04 Contacts", jumps to that part of the page, and the heading isn't hidden under the tabs.

Refresh an out-of-date company page

Some pages are out of date. Open the page from Browse companies, then click "2 Research Urgency" to run fresh web research. This button stays grey until the company has a website. In older screenshots this step is called "Deep research".

Prepare one company

Click "Prepare one" in the menu. Fill in "Company name" and "Domain" (for example embat.io) and click "Prepare outreach". The page takes about 2 to 5 minutes to build, so wait before you click "Open article".

Where the "Sales help" chat appears

The "Sales help" chat is on Browse companies. You can ask it things like "What should I click?" Share lists also has a chat bubble, but that's a general chat.

A watchlist opened as a sales user

A watchlist is a saved group of companies. This is what one looks like after you save it or open it as a sales user.

Part 3: Questions and answers

How do I create a watchlist from a company page?

You can't do it from the company page, because it has no watchlist button. Make the watchlist on Browse companies instead:

  1. Click "Browse companies" in the menu.
  2. Type the company's name or website in the "Search" box and click "Search".
  3. Tick the box in front of the company.
  4. In the bar that appears, click "Save as watchlist".
  5. A "Create watchlist" window opens with a name already filled in. Change the name if you like, then click "Save watchlist".

The watchlist opens straight away, and a share list is made with it. You can tick up to 500 companies before you save. Later, find it under Share lists, because saving also made a share list with the same companies.

There are two other ways to make a watchlist: - Save filtered: set your filters on Browse companies, click "Search", then click "Save filtered (up to 1500)". Everything your filters found (up to 1,500 companies) is saved as a new watchlist plus a share list, with a name filled in for you. - Prepare one: for a company that isn't in Leadtrigger yet, click "Prepare one", fill in "Company name" and "Domain", and click "Prepare outreach". The result box has an "Ad-hoc watchlist" link. You don't choose a name for that list.
